# Selfirm

Selfirm is the headquarters of a company of one: one record of what a person
running a business on their own holds, owes and must do, how those things
relate, and who may act on them. It is built first for software and AI
products, consulting and agencies of one, creators, and e-commerce brands.

- **Checked, not guessed.** Every figure traces back to the statement it came
  from, and balances are checked within and across statements.
- **Files in, no bank connection.** Selfirm reads bank, card and
  payment-account statements as PDF, XLSX, XLS or CSV. It holds no bank
  credentials and cannot move money.
- **Yours and the company's, kept apart.** Every account and movement belongs
  to you or to your company, and transfers between your own accounts are
  paired, never counted as income.
- **Built for agents.** AI agents are meant to work from this record within
  permissions you grant. That layer is planned; see the roadmap.

Finance is the first department. Selfirm is in early access.
